Shut up and build the future
----------------------------

For roughly 11 years, but likely longer, I've expected there to be flying cars. I was promised flying cars, and instead all I got was slightly neater looking cars. Either the timeline to a technological singularity in gravity breaking technology is still far away, or it's just that -- science fiction.

A ubiquitous web is not science fiction though. Not at more, and not so long as there are those dedicated towards it's outcome. I don't want your talk of reducing costs, your talk of making the world more equal while driving up the cost curves of production of all technology as we use AI to eliminate the very people who's creativity helped fuel the market place (looking at you web and big CMS).

Yes, we use AI. AI is helping power immense improvements in our platform, security, and adding features that appear to have been written by me (see [The Many Mes](hax/the-many-mes)).

What you won't see AI do in this community is replace the creativity, spirit and foundational pillars that power this community. HAX is about expanding human flourishing by eliminating the barriers between the technology and human expression.

Yes, that does mean there are jobs we won't need as we attack the "bottom of the pyramid" aka 80+% of all web publishing. We won't need anyone that is between you and your ideas on the screen.

Evidence of an impending utopia
-------------------------------

Yesterday in a new talk (to be published on Apereo's website in coming days) I spent approximately 5 minutes to achieve the following. No smoke and mirrors, other than having a copy of HAX setup on my computer and a copy on hax.psu.edu :

*   Copied the URL of a [Pressbooks](https://psu.pb.unizin.org/socialpsychmethodsjmc948/) site (Wordpress + plugins + constraints = high ed OER platform) and turned it into a HAX site by pasting it into a field
*   Took that site, opened it, drag and dropped a `.DOCX` file into the page and hydrated it into an outline of content
*   Adding `.md` to pages (try it on this blog post) and illustrated that everything is written in HTML but is primed for llm ingestion by default via a managed [llms.txt](https://llmstxt.org/) file
*   Downloaded the site as a zip after making a few edits via HAX as HTML editor
*   Ran `[hax start](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@haxtheweb/create)` locally to make more changes to the site
*   Then ran it again and selected to [publish the site to surge which you can find here](https://earthy-hax-og.surge.sh/)

This is the future we are building. These are our flying cars. Elimination of the barriers between information being in one place in one format and another. HAX has begun investing in multiple converters to simplify migration (as much as possible) to copy and paste so that we can convert anything from its present format into JSON Outline Schema (api that powers hax's site.json files).
