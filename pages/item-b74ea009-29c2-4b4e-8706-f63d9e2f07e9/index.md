Shut up and build (the future)
------------------------------

For roughly 11 years, but likely longer, I've expected there to be flying cars. I was promised flying cars, and instead all I got was slightly neater looking cars. Either the timeline to a technological singularity in gravity breaking technology is still far away, or it's just that -- science fiction.

Replace flying cars with web development that reduced costs, improved outcomes, had high up-times, was easy to use, migrate, and extend. Those flying cars have gotten incrementally better each year - sure. There's no denying WordPress, Drupal, Grav, the other big proprietary vendors we won't mention -- have all gotten better.

Incrementally better. n+1. via ai maybe n+2

Ubiquity is n^2 not n+2
-----------------------

A ubiquitous web is not science fiction if we lean in and make it a reality. Not at more, and not so long as there are those dedicated towards it's outcome. I don't want your talk of reducing costs, your talk of making the world more equal while driving up the cost curves of production, using AI as integrated pieces of your platforms not to solve UX issues but instead to mask them. To burn all the earth's resources because you didn't feel like making a design pattern for your users and just told them all a bar that says "what do you want to do" was more efficient (it's not).

Yes, we use AI. AI is helping power immense improvements in our platform, security, and adding features that appear to have been written by me (see [The Many Mes](hax/the-many-mes)). But we use AI to finish the human work faster and the training data has been how humans are building with HAX but also what those who founded HAX think. We want ethically built, future looking, destructive innovation. Not n+3. n^2 then n^3 then n^o. Destructive portability. Free flowing information supplamented by AI, not forever powered by it.

What you won't see AI do in this community is replace the creativity, spirit and foundational pillars that power this community. HAX is about expanding human flourishing by eliminating the barriers between the technology and human expression. AI helps us get the job done faster for sure -- but always open, always decentralized, always sovereign in output.

Yes, that does mean there are jobs we won't need as we attack the "bottom of the pyramid" aka 80+% of all web publishing. We won't need anyone that is between you and your ideas on the screen. That means your ideas can be liberated and those in the transactional layer to get your ideas to the screen will also be freed up to do more interesting work. No one wakes up and says "damn, so excited to write another YAML file so we can deploy that top level domain a little faster today". Maybe in 2016 when this was new -- but it's not and we must break stagnation not sit and profit off it's complexity.

Evidence of an impending utopia
-------------------------------

Yesterday in a new talk (to be published on Apereo's website in coming days) I spent approximately 5 minutes to achieve the following. No smoke and mirrors, other than having a copy of HAX setup on my computer and a copy on hax.psu.edu :

*   Copied the URL of a [Pressbooks](https://psu.pb.unizin.org/socialpsychmethodsjmc948/) site (Wordpress + plugins + constraints = high ed OER platform) and turned it into a HAX site by pasting it into a field
*   Took that site, opened it, drag and dropped a `.DOCX` file into the page and hydrated it into an outline of content
*   Adding `.md` to pages (try it on this blog post) and illustrated that everything is written in HTML but is primed for llm ingestion by default via a managed [llms.txt](https://llmstxt.org/) file
*   Downloaded the site as a zip after making a few edits via HAX as HTML editor
*   Ran `[hax start](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@haxtheweb/create)` locally to make more changes to the site
*   Then ran it again and selected to [publish the site to surge which you can find here](https://earthy-hax-og.surge.sh/)

This is the future we are building.

These are our flying cars.

Elimination of the barriers between information being in one place in one format and another; and Pressbooks is just one example of the many methods we're adding to break the the barrier between your ideas in the fomats they are and the formats they need to be for being on the web.

HAX has begun investing in multiple converters to simplify migration (as much as possible) to copy and paste so that we can convert anything from its present format into [JSON Outline Schema](https://github.com/haxtheweb/json-outline-schema) (api that powers [hax's site.json](https://btopro.com/site.json) files). Added in this release alone, is support for importing pptx, docx, xlsx, pdf, pressbooks, and many others if the right APIs are exposed (Drupal, WordPress, Plone, among them). We want to eliminate the barrier between where something is and where it could be; but also who makes that happen. Months to minutes. High skill elite only capabilities democratized and simplified for anyone to go "I want That thing to be over here" and it happens.
