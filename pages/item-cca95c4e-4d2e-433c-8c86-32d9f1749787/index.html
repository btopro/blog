Another year and I'm back in Portland to learn more about community development and management. This year I feel a bit more well equipped for the talks thus far from lessons learned last time. Some wild changes since last time I was here a year ago:

  • We have a lab (HAX Lab)
  • We have a student HAX Club that's formed
  • We have a faculty collaborator that's directly working to grow the project (and has secured 1 grant thus far to fund student development with more on the way)
  • We have a security reporting pipeline
  • We have a HAX CLI and a bunch more themes

In fact, it's kind of ridiculous how much community investment we've had since the last time I was here. As a result, I'm back to present this picture.

In the talk I'll break out the trends I found from creating this and most importantly, highlight lessons learned from a year of HAX Lab development and 10 years of taking HAX from an idea on a notepad to where we are today.
