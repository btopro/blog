Refinement

Now it feels like in coming here that I'm in the refinement phase. We have a lot of community investment, growth, and major wins the last year; now coming here I don't feel like I have new eyes but eyes that at least have seen this space. I want to take our community to the next level and so I am here to learn the small ways I can improve upon what we have

new things to incorporate

  • SLAs - We did this awhile ago, need to bring it back
  • Maker concept -- incentivizing Build and share among the community
  • Value comes from an ecosystem, not the thing itself. How do we best communicate value of the ecosystem? Probably through themes
  • "Content enabling products are not solutions on their own"
  • Create open office hours weekly for asking questions about HAX. Do this on discord via open audio channel
  • Add a "good first issue" category to github and highlight it. Treat this like it's the best way into the ecosystem. Could even roll this into class if they are ACTAULLY 1st issues
  • Help people find intrinsic motivation as to why they'd be involved
  • Make a 30 second video of each README file for the big 5
  • Story telling is a critical skill -- and we have an awesome story that's building, we just need to keep telling it
  • As the Club expands, identify clear and distinct roles within our project
  • HAX Club sprints as HAX Maker Space. It's not about the product, it's about what you can build / create with the product. We need to create a space to be creative in order to cultivate an ecosystem

It's all about the ecosystem!
