in zone coverage

Coverage in zone to start this clip is solid but could use a few improvements

  1. Sam locking up the O in the corner is great. Gage is drifting toward the play but should be joining to help Sam instead of waiting for him to win the battle. OR covering the circle
  2. Mason is along the boards. At the stop of the clip him and Gage are basically covering the same ice so we need to space that better. Tenley is in decent position, Bella could stand to be a bit more toward that hash mark but I'm fine with where she is as the play just previously went wide below the goal line

  • Bella's prior gap proves effective. The player from the corner attempts to hit someone filling the circle because of the gap left by Gage / Mason. Tenley also reads the play but gets the a little late. Bella's reach and recognition allows her to break up the play
  • Gage and Mason recognize this immediately and start to turn their feet to exit the zone
  • Bella makes a great pass while intercepting all in one motion and Mason is ready to receive the pass

  • Mason picks his head up, recognizes that the defender is too close to the wall and attacks the middle / opposite side of the ice where there's less people.
  • Gage exhibits good knee bend to attempt to attempt to keep up.
  • The other team backchecks very effectively to make up for their mistake. You can see 3 players have hustled over to take away Mason's ice and Isolate him
  • Also of note, Both D and a wing are still in the zone watching. Regardless of the wing not activating, the D need to not watch and join the rush. Where the AHA logo is on the ice is plenty of ice to take. Puck watching is what limits chances overall on this play

  • Mason hits the line, pulls up realizing he doesn't have support and is outnumbered. He throws a relatively harmless shot on goal
  • During this sequence, Gage previously is rushing into the zone and slows up entering the zone. If he continues attacking the net he would be first to the puck or possibly in position for a rebound. If entering the zone out numbered, gain ice, but then create space by 1 person ALWAYS crashing the net. Don't stop because you think you might get a pass.
  • Mason falls down on release. it's been awhile but we'll have to keep working on this motion this season :)

  • The rebound kicks to the corner, Gage follows the player behind the net.
  • The D player makes a critical mistake of scooping up the puck and going to their backhand. Gage recognizes this and attacks the player's stick.
  • The player escapes but is slowed up and goes into the corner on their strong side.
  • Tenley enters the play but is set up for a shooting position. Right now her and Mason occupy the same ice
  • Bella is slowly entering the zone, needs to get their faster and communicate to Ten/Mason to move in. Sammy is using this time to get a line-change which is a smart play.

  • Gage attacks player, gains leverage and forces him to just clear up the wall
  • Mason recognizes the play, stops the puck along the boards, makes a nice move down the boards. Gage is at the dot which isn't a terrible spot, but someone needs to be crashing the net
  • Tenley is still surveying the play and needs to either be right in front of the net messing with the defender, or below the goal as an option to pass
  • Jake off on the far right side is covered but COULD move into the slot as the ice is completely open there.
  • Mason is looking for a pass / to make a move but can't find one as a result of these 3 issues

  • Mason, the penalty magnet draws another one by getting leveled while trying to make a move
  • A second Fox comes into the corner and Gage ties his stick up to attempt to get another play. There are 3 separate mini-interference by Gage that are great as far as putting yourself in the play to bug someone else, could be a very ticky-tacky penalty, but go on called due to how quick they happen.
  • Jake / Bella are still in surveillance mode and not pass options though the ref's hand is up behind them. If the ref's hand goes up for an obvious penalty against the other team, then the D posture should be VERY aggressive. If you are unsure, remain, but on obvious penalties you can begin to take huge risks without consequence.

Take aways

  • We scored on the very next shift on the powerplay.
  • That does not happen, powerplay, zone time, without two smart and simple plays by Mason and Gage
  • It's not always about scoring that shift and single bad passes or decisions can completely send the play the other direction
  • If you don't know what to do on D, fallback back to the house isn't a bad option but make sure not to double cover the same ice
  • If you don't know what to do on O, crashing the net isn't a bad option; just make sure you are not double covering ice.
  • D should consider themselves part of the play. Don't watch, as it's going safely the other way you need to engage more.
  • Minor adjustments and maybe we score on the initial play but this game overall wall filled with solid efforts by all lines
